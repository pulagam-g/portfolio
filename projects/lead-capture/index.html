How the workflow runs

01 TriggerWebhook

The website form posts name, email and message to n8n.

02 CleanSet & validate

Trims fields, checks the email and adds a timestamp.

03 StoreGoogle Sheets

Appends the lead as a new row in one clean list.

04 NotifyOwner alert

Emails the lead details to the business owner instantly.

05 ReplyAuto-reply

Thanks the visitor and sets expectations for a reply.
