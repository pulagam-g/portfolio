Lead-capture automation

Every website enquiry lands in a Google Sheet, the business owner gets an instant alert, and the visitor gets a friendly auto-reply. No copy-pasting, no missed leads.

How it works

  1. Webhook: the website form posts name, email and message to an n8n webhook.
  2. Set & validate: trims fields, checks the email format and adds a timestamp and source.
  3. Google Sheets: appends the lead as a new row, so the owner has one clean list.
  4. Email to owner: sends an instant alert with the lead details.
  5. Auto-reply: thanks the visitor and tells them when to expect an answer.

Built with n8n, Google Sheets and Gmail nodes. This page simulates the run in the browser; no data is sent anywhere.
